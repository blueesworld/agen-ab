AAgen

Black. One piece.

Nothing gets in the way.

AgenPhone 1 is milled from a single block of recycled titanium and finished in an impossibly deep Matte Black. No seams. No physical buttons. Only the essentials.

Touch without a button.

The edge, reimagined.

A responsive haptic edge senses pressure, slides and gestures. It gives you the satisfying certainty of a button, with nothing to interrupt the form.

A new point of view.

Dual 48MP Fusion camera.

More light. More detail. More of the moments that only happen once. The new Fusion system combines two pro cameras into a single effortless experience.
